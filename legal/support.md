# Support

**Whiskered — Mewgenics Companion**

## Need help?

If you're having issues with Whiskered or have a question, here's how to get help:

### Common issues

**Camera not working**
Make sure you've granted camera permission in Settings > Whiskered > Camera.

**Scanner not recognizing items**
- Hold your device steady and make sure the item is centered in the viewfinder
- Use the +/- buttons to adjust the frame size
- Ensure good lighting — avoid glare on your screen
- Try picking a screenshot from your photo library instead

**App crashing or freezing**
Try force-closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, make sure you're running the latest version from the App Store.
